The one big excursion: a misty mountain of palaces half an hour from the city. Pick two, not five, keep it slow, and be back for the anniversary dinner.

Plan B🔭
☔If it rains: Sintra is often in cloud even when Lisbon isn't — that's the magic, not a problem. Pena's interiors and the Quinta's grottoes are wonderful wet.

Getting around

Wonderfully walkable, brutally hilly. Trams and lifts exist precisely because of the hills.

Metro — 4 clean, fast lines€1.80 / ride
Tram & bus — Slower, prettier, the 28 is a sight in itself€3 on board
Rideshare — Uber & Bolt, cheap and everywhere~€6 across town

From the airport: Metro red line to the centre in 20 minutes (~€1.80), or a €15 Bolt straight to the hotel with five people and a dog — worth it on arrival day.

Parking: Don't rent a car for the city. You'll only want one for Sintra, and the train is better anyway.
