Vacayi is for people who don't like planning a vacation out, but still like having plans.

For people who stress about creating the perfect trip, or about having to plan every single piece of it. For anyone with multiple factors to consider when they travel — and for families who just want something convenient and personalized.

The point of vacayi is to let the app learn about you, then correlate every bit of information it has to build a complete trip guide around your preferences. That's what makes planning a vacation effortless: the bulk of it is already done, in accordance with what you actually like. Some things will need tweaking — but the intention is that you trust its selections and follow the guide.

It's built to be your one stop shop. Designed to feel like you took the time to create it yourself, and to be something you and the family will genuinely have fun with.

And for the people who get stuck bored on a weekend, wishing they had a full day planned out with the family — that's what it's there for too.

One guide, everything booked

Book the whole trip without leaving the guide.

vacayi works out what your trip actually needs — the flight, the hotels, the tours worth pre-booking, a ride from the airport, data for your phone — and lines it up in one place with a tap to book each one.

  • ✓Nothing invented. It only lists what your own plan calls for, and never claims you've booked something you haven't.
  • ✓Tick it off as you go. The ring fills, the trip gets sorted, and it all syncs across everyone on the trip.
  • ✓Your official links come first. Where a place sells direct, we send you there — even when it earns us nothing.

While you're there, and long after

The photos find their own way home.

Take pictures the way you always do. vacayi reads where and when each one was taken and drops it onto the right stop, on the right day — no tagging, no sorting, no album to build afterwards. By the time you land home the trip has written itself back into the guide.

  • ✓It happens by itself. A photo taken at the viewpoint lands on the viewpoint. The day comes first and the place second, so turning up two hours late — or on a completely different afternoon — still finds the right stop.
  • ✓Nothing leaves your phone. No upload, no copy on our servers, nothing to sign away. Delete a picture and it's gone from the guide with it.
  • ✓You end up with something to show people. Not a camera roll of four hundred pictures — the whole trip in order, day by day, with your photos already sitting where they happened and every quest somebody won. Open it at the table and walk them through it.
