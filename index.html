Before you go

Your trip, one day at a time.

Not a spreadsheet of times. Each day is paced for your crew — where to go, when, and why it's worth it — with the hours, the addresses and the way there.

  • Paced to the people goingA full day for the ones who want it, a slower one when a toddler or a grandparent says so.

Every booking, one place

Drop in a confirmation. The guide does the rest.

Add a flight, a hotel or a ticket and it lands in the wallet with the times, the addresses and the confirmation code — and the days reshape around it.

  • Flights set the trip's windowLand at 3 PM and the first day starts when you do.

Straight talk

No invented facts.

An AI that makes things up is worse than a guidebook. vacayi is built to say only what it can stand behind.

  • Places are checked against live map data.Every stop is looked up as a real place, and any we couldn't confirm are marked on their card.
  • Hours, prices and phone numbers are flagged to verify — never made up.The guide tells you what to double-check, and when.
  • When it isn't sure, it says so.Uncertain stays uncertain; nothing is dressed up as fact.
  • Your crew's details stay yours.People you share a trip with read the guide — they never get your crew's saved profiles: ages, allergies or health notes.
  • Photos stay on your phone.Matching happens on the device; nothing uploads until you share.

Questions

Questions people ask.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT, or reading TripAdvisor?

A chat gives you a list; a review site gives you everyone's opinion. vacayi builds one guide for your dates, your crew and your pace, with real places checked against map data — then carries it through the trip: bookings, map, quests and all.

Can I change the plan?

Yes. Tap the planner inside the guide and say what you want — add a stop, swap a restaurant, move a day. It edits the guide and keeps everything else consistent.

Does it work without a signal?

Download the guide before you go and it reads offline. Live parts like the forecast update when you're back online.

Can the people I'm travelling with see it?

Invite them to the trip. Everyone reads the same guide in their own language, can comment and favourite, and editors can change the plan. Only the person who created the guide can open the crew's saved profiles.

What happens to my kids' ages and allergies?

They shape the guide and nothing else. They're stored for your account only, never shown to other travellers on a shared trip, and you can delete everything from the app or at vacayi.app/delete-data.

Which phones?

iPhone and Android. Both are coming soon — tap Tell me when it's out and we'll email you when it's out.

Which languages?

Guides are written in English, Português (Brasil) or Español, and a shared guide reads in each traveller's own language.
